## Slide 2 — Define it (01)
- H: A skill is a **folder that teaches Claude a job.**
- Body: It bundles a `SKILL.md` (the instructions) + optional scripts, templates, and data. That's it — mostly plain English.
- Card: folder tree `my-skill/ → SKILL.md, scripts/, assets/`

## Slide 3 — How it works (02)
- H: Claude loads it **only when it's relevant.**
- Body: Name + description stay visible; the full instructions and files load on demand. So you can have dozens of skills without clogging the context.
- Visual: flow — your request → Claude matches the skill → loads instructions + tools → does the job your way.

## Slide 4 — Where to get them (03)
- H: Three places to get skills.
- ① Official — `github.com/anthropics/skills` (canvas-design, pdf, pptx, xlsx…)
- ② Build your own — it's just a folder + a `SKILL.md`. No coding required.
- ③ Community & marketplaces — plugins, shared repos, `/plugin`.
- Note: drop them in `.claude/skills/` (Claude Code) or upload on claude.ai.

## Slide 5 — Why you'd want them (04)
- H: Why bother?
- ① Consistency — Claude does it *your* way, every time.
- ② Stop re-explaining — package the prompt once.
- ③ Real power — bundle scripts, fonts, API flows. Not just text.
- ④ Portable — share across your team and tools.
- Punch: *This entire carousel was generated by a skill.*
