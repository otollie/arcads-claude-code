So… what is it?
Markdown is a simple way to format plain text.
You add small symbols like # or ** while you type, and they turn into headings, bold, and lists when the text is displayed. A file saved with a .md ending is simply text written this way.
Think of it as the easy, readable cousin of HTML — power without the tags.
