# Business config — IAVRS

Source: official Brand Guide v1.0 2026 (92-page PDF, uploaded 2026-07-03). Full name:
International Immigration Application Visa Referral Service. **Attorney matching
service, NOT a law firm** — always describe it that way, never imply legal advice.

## Identity
- Handle: `@iavrs`
- Positioning line: "Attorney matching service, not a law firm · United States Visas"
- Audience: people worldwide seeking U.S. work/talent visas (E-2, EB-2 NIW, O-1)

## Logo
- Master seal: `carousel/brand/iavrs/seal.png` (1024px, transparent, extracted from guide)
- Companion star mark (tight spaces): `carousel/brand/iavrs/star-mark.png`
- Rules: never redraw, recolor, rotate, stretch, or add effects. Place on white or navy
  only. Clear space = height of the inner eagle (~1/4 seal diameter). Use star mark when
  the full seal won't read.
- Lockups: horizontal (wordmark right of seal), stacked (wordmark below), seal only.

## Colors
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Navy (primary) | #1F2B4D |
| Gold (accent) | #C2A24A |
| Dark Gold (small text on light) | #8A6E2A |
| Near-black (body text) | #10202E |
| Muted Gray (secondary text) | #5A6B7A |
| Light Surface (panels) | #F2F4F8 |
| Warm Cream (warm surface) | #F6F4EF |
| White (background) | #FFFFFF |

Balance rule: ~80% white/light surface, 15% navy, 5% gold. Gold is for seals, key lines,
and emphasis CTAs — never large blocks. Light and airy; navy and gold are accents, not fills.

## Typography (both free on Google Fonts)
- Headings: **Libre Caslon Display**, regular, navy
- Body: **Hanken Grotesk** 400/500/600/700, near-black, never below 16px
- Scale ref: Display 64 / H1 38 / H2 24 / Body 16 / Label 13 (gold, tracked caps)

## Components
- Flat and clean: no shadows, no gradients. Buttons 8px radius (primary navy fill/white
  text; secondary white fill/navy text/gray border; emphasis gold fill/navy text).
- Cards: white fill, thin border, 12–14px radius, 24–28px padding.

## Voice
- Authoritative, clear, human. Plain language an 8th grader understands. Specific, never
  vague. Warm — real people and real faces build trust.
- Say: "We match you with a vetted U.S. immigration attorney." "Built for the E-2,
  EB-2 NIW, and O-1." "Real attorneys. Real people. Clear next steps."
- Avoid: vague claims ("world-class solutions"), implying it's a law firm or gives legal
  advice, em dashes, emojis.

## Imagery
- Warm, professional photos of real people in studio/modern-office settings, neutral warm
  backgrounds. Gold seal as trust mark next to proof points.

## Design principles
1. Light and airy 2. Flat and clean 3. Premium yet approachable 4. Mobile-first.

## Default CTA
- "Get matched, free" (gold emphasis button pattern from guide). Confirm per deck.

## Compliance footer (required on social)
- "Attorney matching service, not a law firm" appears with the handle.
